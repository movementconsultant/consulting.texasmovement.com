The B2B funnel, inverted

All the SEO/AEO/GEO/AIO/LLMO jargon rolls up to one reality: you need to be visible, understandable, and credible wherever your buyers ask questions.
Stage Old model Inverted, TMC-style What we build with you
Top of funnel Maximize traffic, keywords, and impressions. Constrain topics and ICP so only serious buyers show up. Clear POV, topic map, and consistent presence in a few key channels.
Middle of funnel Random MQLs and nurture flows. Evidence-backed content that matches how buyers actually research. Case-style narratives, diagnostic tools, and explicit next steps.
Bottom of funnel Messy definitions, broken handoffs, and noisy CRM. Fewer, higher-intent conversations and clean data. Qualification rules, pipeline hygiene, and feedback loops.
Systems layer Each AI tool is its own island. Vendor-agnostic workflows and documentation. Playbooks that survive model changes and team turnover.
