What this page is

This is the execution layer of Texas Movement. It shows how consulting turns complex situations into systems, and how engagements are scoped and documented.

Who it’s for

Founders and operators who think in constraints and want durable execution, not motivation. The work is built for people whose stakes are real.

Teams carrying a complex situation—a launch, a legal matter’s operational side, a workflow that keeps breaking—that need it turned into clear decisions and steps.

It is a fit when the brief is clear enough to scope. It is not a fit for open-ended “help us figure out everything” with no context.

How I work

Systems first. Structure, timelines, and ownership come before tactics or narrative.

Document before narrative. The record must hold up under review—internally and externally.

Legally in-bounds. Work supports your counsel; it does not act as counsel or provide legal advice.

Built for serious work. Engagements are scoped to real constraints and outcomes, not vague wishes.

Engagements

Systems audit

I map your current workflow, locate the friction, and return a prioritized plan—timelines, decisions, and owners. You get a clear picture of what to fix first and why, in a form you can execute against immediately.

AI-workflow build

I design and stand up GPT, Claude, Gemini-assisted workflows that reduce friction and improve execution, grounded in your actual constraints and tools.

Ops decision support

I turn a complex situation into clear decisions, sequencing, and a record that holds up under review so you are not deciding in a fog.

Embedded retainer

Ongoing execution support for founders who need a systems partner across lanes—strategy, workflows, and documentation over time.

Frequently asked questions

What kind of work is this?

Strategy, systems design, AI-assisted workflows, and implementation support. It is structure and execution, not slideware.

Who is it a fit for?

Founders, operators, and teams with real stakes and a clear-enough brief. It is a poor fit for vague, no-context requests.

Do you provide legal advice?

No. Work stays in-bounds and supports your counsel; it does not replace it or act as legal representation.

How does an engagement start?

Send a brief with context, constraints, and what a good outcome looks like. The first step is scope and fit, not a pitch.

How is this different from generic consulting?

It is systems-first and documented, built to compound with the rest of Texas Movement rather than sit as a one-off project.
