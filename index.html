The method

The B2B funnel, inverted

SEO, AEO, GEO, AIO, LLMO — all the acronyms roll up to one reality: be visible, understandable, and credible wherever your buyers ask questions. Less random traffic, more reliable pipeline.

Stage Old model Inverted, TMC-style What we build with you
Top Maximize traffic, keywords, and impressions. Constrain topics and ICP so only serious buyers show up. Clear POV, topic map, consistent presence in a few channels.
Middle Random MQLs and generic nurture flows. Evidence-backed content that matches how buyers research. Case-style narratives, diagnostic assets, explicit next steps.
Bottom Messy definitions, broken handoffs, noisy CRM. Fewer, higher-intent conversations and clean data. Qualification rules, pipeline hygiene, feedback loops.
Systems Each AI tool is its own island; outages hurt. Vendor-agnostic workflows and documentation. Playbooks that survive model changes and team turnover.
