FAQ

Frequently asked questions.

What kind of work is this?

Strategy, systems design, AI-assisted workflows, and implementation support. It is structure and execution, not motivational speaking or slide decks.

Who is it a fit for?

Founders, operators, and teams with real stakes and a clear brief. It is a poor fit for vague, no-context requests or projects with no decision-maker.

Do you provide legal advice?

No. Consulting stays in-bounds and supports your counsel; it does not replace legal advice or representation.

How does an engagement start?

Send a brief with context, constraints, and what a good outcome looks like. First step is scope and fit—not a generic discovery call.

How is this different from generic consulting?

It is systems-first and documented, built to compound with the rest of Texas Movement rather than sit as a one-off. The record is part of the deliverable.
