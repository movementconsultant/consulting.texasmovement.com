How We Work

Every project moves through the same spine: Diagnose → Design → Implement → Transfer.

01 · Diagnose

Map how work, information, and money actually move today. Find where load is leaking and where founder dependence is highest.

02 · Design

Decide which 2–3 systems matter most: pipeline, intake, scheduling, delivery, follow‑up. Design workflows that degrade safely under load.

03 · Implement

Build the flows, automations, and SOPs with your existing tools where possible, adding AI only where it meaningfully compresses work.

04 · Transfer

Document decisions, train internal owners, and set a simple operating cadence so the system runs without consultant dependence.

AI as amplifier, not theater. We use AI to speed up documentation, decision support, and routine communication — not to turn your business into a science project.
