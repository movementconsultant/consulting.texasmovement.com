What we do

Three domains. One method: diagnose, rebuild, transfer.

Every project starts with a structural diagnostic and ends with a system that can run without the founder in the room.

01 · AI systems

AI projects that ship and hold.

Most AI builds fail on structure, not models. We map the constraints, data paths, and failure modes before you commit serious capital.

  • Scope and constraint mapping before a line of code.
  • Data and workflow architecture that degrades safely.
  • Playbooks so the team can operate without the vendor.
02 · Funnels & ops

B2B funnels that match the org behind them.

The funnel, delivery, and operations are one system. We find where load is being lost and redesign the path from lead to cash.

  • Funnel architecture and bottleneck diagnosis.
  • Handoffs and SOPs that remove single points of failure.
  • Operating cadence the team owns, not the founder.
03 · Performance systems

Performance holds under real load.

Biomechanics-grade diagnostics applied to teams and individuals who need to perform repeatedly, not just once.

  • Force-leak mapping and return-to-load logic.
  • Protocols designed to be run by staff, not just experts.
  • Documentation so the system survives turnover.
04 · Transfer

The method leaves with you.

The end state is not a report; it’s a system. We build the documentation, decision rules, and training so the work compounds.

  • System playbooks and decision trees.
  • Role-level training and enablement.
  • Options for certification and internal ownership.
Every engagement moves left to right. We’re done when your team can run the system without us.
